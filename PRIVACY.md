# Privacy Policy — LL Fruit Squash

**Effective date:** November 12, 2026
**Last updated:** November 12, 2026

LL Fruit Squash ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is a physics-based fruit-merging puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not collect, how it is handled, and the rights you have.

**Short version: LL Fruit Squash does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. It works entirely offline. All game data is stored locally on your device.**

---

## 1. Data We Collect

**We do not collect any personal data or user information.**

The App does not access, gather, transmit, or store any of the following:

- Name, email address, phone number, or postal address
- Contacts, calendar, photos, or files
- Precise or coarse location
- Health, fitness, or biometric data
- Financial or payment information
- Browsing history, search history, or app usage analytics
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA), device identifiers (IDFV), or any other unique identifiers
- Diagnostics, crash logs, or performance telemetry
- Microphone, camera, or any other sensor data beyond local motion sensing described below

The App contains **no analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs**, and **no third-party libraries** that collect data. It does not perform any network requests at runtime. A small "Privacy Policy" link on the start screen opens this document in your device's web browser; that navigation is initiated by you and handled entirely by the browser, not by the App.

### Local motion sensor use

The App reads the device accelerometer, through Apple's CoreMotion framework, solely to detect a shake gesture that lets you settle the fruit pile. This motion data is processed **in real time on your device only**. It is never recorded, stored, transmitted, or shared.

## 2. Collection Methods

We do not collect data, so there are no collection methods to disclose. The App does not use cookies, identifiers, accounts, or server-side logging.

---

## 3. How Data Is Used

Because no data is collected, no user data is used or processed for any purpose — including marketing, analytics, personalization, or profiling. All gameplay logic runs locally on your device.

---

## 4. Data Stored on Your Device

To provide basic game functionality, the App saves the following preferences and game state **locally on your device** using Apple's `UserDefaults` storage:

| Stored item | Purpose |
|---|---|
| High score | Display and track your best score |
| Mute setting | Remember your sound preference |
| Haptics setting | Remember your haptic feedback preference |
| Theme selection | Remember your chosen visual theme |

This information:

- Never leaves your device.
- Is not linked to your identity or to any account.
- Is not visible to us or to any third party.
- Is **not** synced to iCloud or any other cloud service.

You can erase all locally stored data at any time by deleting the App from your device.

---

## 5. Third-Party Sharing

**We do not share any data with third parties**, because we do not collect any.

The App does not integrate advertising networks, analytics providers, data brokers, or other third-party SDKs. It does not display targeted advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Apple may independently process limited information as part of the operating system and app distribution (for example, App Store or AltStore installation), under Apple's or the distributor's own privacy policies. That processing is outside our control and is not performed by the App.

---

## 6. Data Retention

We retain no user data on any server or backend, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Locally stored preferences remain on your device until you change them or delete the App. Uninstalling the App removes this local data.

---

## 7. Your Rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct, export, or delete on our side.

- **Consent / revocation:** The App requires no consent for data collection because it collects none.
- **Deletion:** You can delete all locally stored data by removing the App from your device.
- **Requests:** If you believe we hold information about you, or you have any privacy question or request, contact us at the address below and we will respond promptly.

Depending on your jurisdiction (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal data. Since we do not collect or process personal data, these rights are satisfied by design; you may still contact us with any concerns.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region). Because no data is collected, the App is designed to be compliant with children's privacy laws such as COPPA.

## 9. Security

As no data is transmitted or stored off-device, there is no server-side data to secure. Locally stored preferences are protected by your device's built-in security, including device passcode and OS-level sandboxing.
